## US-1 — A written environment matrix, with each cell's verdict and how it was reached

Added `docs/reference/environment-matrix.md` and linked it from `docs/README.md`. The matrix
covers iOS 17+ native and pre-17 TUSKit transports, Android 14/15 workers, platform source types,
and four interruption modes. Each cell names `AUTOMATED`, `MANUAL-ON-DEVICE`, or `NOT YET VERIFIED`,
with physical-device-only gaps called out rather than replaced by simulator evidence. The document
also records the checked-in code-path evidence for both iOS transports and the evidence needed to
close a manual cell.

Verification: `.chief/verify.sh` passed Swift build, Swift tests (`Amphora path tests: 44 passed`),
and TypeScript typecheck; Gradle was skipped because no Gradle toolchain was available.

### Learnings for future iterations

Environmental evidence must distinguish checked-in code-path tests from physical-device behavior;
simulators/emulators cannot substantiate background budgets, process death, storage reclamation, or
radio transitions. Keep the matrix cells explicit so later device runs can replace only the relevant
verdicts.
